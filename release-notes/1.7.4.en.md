BaoCode Codex 1.7.4: a first turn on your own upstream works

In the previous release, if you had picked one of your own upstreams in
Settings, the first message crashed the conversation with a
LateInitializationError. That step had no test; this release fixes it.

Fixed
- Picking one of your own upstreams no longer breaks the conversation: the
  app's internal state was initialized a second time by the switch, and can
  be replaced now; the first message goes to the upstream you picked.
- A scripted transport under test is kept, no longer thrown away for one
  from the factory.
