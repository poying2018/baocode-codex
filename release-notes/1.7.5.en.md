BaoCode Codex 1.7.5: switching upstreams no longer collides

The previous release gave each of your own upstreams a Codex process of its
own - but both processes shared one state folder, and locked each other out:
picking one of your models and sending a message said "another Codex may be
running".

Fixed
- Each upstream's server keeps its state in a folder of its own (seeded from
  the main one); switching upstreams no longer collides, and switching back
  to the default works as before.
- At quit the app waits fifteen seconds for its servers to go, not three: an
  upgrade can no longer race one that is still writing its databases out -
  the other source of the error after an upgrade.
