BaoCode Codex 1.7.10: picking one of your own models shows as picked

1.7.9 made the model in effect the upstream's own short name everywhere -
the picker's checked state included. The picker's option names are
references (@provider/...), which never equal the short name, so nothing
you picked ever showed as picked: it looked like the custom models could
not be chosen at all.

Fixed
- What the picker sees: your pick as it was made (the reference) - the
  checked state is right from now on.
- What the server receives: the model as the upstream serves it -
  converted only when a request is actually made.
