BaoCode Codex 1.7.6: a failed start says what really happened

"Codex ended before it was ready / Another Codex may be running" was a
guess: every early exit said it, whether or not a folder was held, and
the real cause stayed hidden.

Improved
- What Codex says on stderr is now kept beside its state
  (codex-stderr.log) and carried in the error - the real reason is right
  there (a lock, a config, or something else).
- The folder-lock wording appears only when Codex's own words say a lock
  or a database was held.
- A start that ends is tried once more, after a quarter of a second: a
  maintenance pass or a file still being written out is over by then.
